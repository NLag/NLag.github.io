About

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the DAVID Lab, UVSQ (Université Paris-Saclay), where I work on network security for 5G and 6G. My current work brings quantum machine learning into intrusion detection, with hybrid quantum-classical classifiers for the 5G Core. My PhD, defended in December 2025, built an AI-based intrusion detection system for IoT traffic that follows the 3GPP standard and runs inside the 5G Core.

I'm also technical lead at Tihado, where I take AI products from idea to working prototype: voice-first learning tools, fitness coaching, video automation and robot control. Before that I studied cryptography and information security in Limoges, and I still compete in the NSUCRYPTO cryptography olympiad.
